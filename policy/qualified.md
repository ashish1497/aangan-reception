# Which enquiries are worth a designer's time

This is the policy the desk applies to every call. Nikhil owns it. The numbers sit in
`policy/rubric.json`; change them there and the desk follows.

## What we take on

- Homes and small offices.
- Projects in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.
- Homes of at least 400 sq ft. Offices of at least 300 sq ft.
- A budget of Rs 5 lakh or more, when the caller is willing to say. A caller who does not
  give a budget is not turned away for it.

## How a call is decided

- **Qualified:** the project is ours, the size and the budget are in range. It goes to a
  designer with the brief, and a call is booked before the caller hangs up.
- **Grey:** we are not sure. The size is within 20 percent of the minimum, the budget is just
  under, a detail is missing, or the caller was upset. The front desk looks at it the next
  working morning. A good lead is never closed on a guess.
- **Unqualified:** the project is outside what we do or where we work, or clearly below the
  minimum. The caller is thanked politely and the call, the reason and the transcript are
  kept.

## Second look

Every unqualified call gets a second look rating. A **near miss** is one that failed only on
size or budget and reached at least half the minimum. A near miss is worth one quick human
check before it is closed. A project outside Pune is never a near miss.

## What a designer should never have to ask again

Name, project type, area, location, budget range, timeline, and the slot the caller chose.
