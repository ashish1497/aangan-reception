# What the desk says about price

The desk gives a range, never one number, and always says it varies by site.

| Project | Per sq ft            |
| ------- | -------------------- |
| Home    | Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,000 |
| Office  | Rs 900 to Rs 1,800   |

Wording on the call and on the dashboard: "Indicative only, varies by site. A designer
confirms the estimate after seeing the space."

The numbers sit in `policy/rubric.json`.
